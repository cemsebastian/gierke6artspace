Four rooms.
Many possibilities.

Gierke6 Art Space is a versatile venue in the heart of Berlin-Charlottenburg. Across roughly 100 m², four rooms with clean lines and real character provide the right setting for exhibitions, presentations, workshops, seminars, castings, and photo and film productions.

The two spacious main rooms offer distinctive sightlines and plenty of freedom to stage a project your own way. Flexible ceiling hanging points let you present art, installations, lighting and unconventional setups.

The rooms can be arranged and styled to suit each project, and adapted to different formats. A kitchen and a separate backstage area round off the space and provide the infrastructure that artists, teams and guests need. The rooms can be rented by the day or for longer periods.
